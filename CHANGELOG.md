# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- `<x-hibira-whos-here />`: live avatars of the signed-in people on a page or room.
- `<livewire:hibira-batch-progress :batch="$id" />`: job batch progress
  without `wire:poll`, broadcast after each batched job when the percentage,
  failures or finished state change; `hibira-batch-finished` event.
- `<x-hibira-connection-status />`: realtime connection light.
- Texts in en, ja, ko, zh_TW, zh_CN, th, vi.
